FAQ
Q
Do I need a Google Fitbit Air?

Yes. The sensor pebble is not included with your PIV watch. You remove it from your existing Fitbit Air band and slide it into the bracket. It pops in and out easily, so you can swap back whenever you like.

Q
Does the Fitbit Air work the same way?

Yes, fully. Heart rate, sleep tracking, and steps all sync through the Google Fit app as normal. Nothing changes on the software side.

Q
What do you sell?

Three products: the PIV Band + Bracket ($29.99) which fits any 18mm or 20mm lug watch you already own, the PIV F-91 ($99.99) based on the Casio F-91W, and the PIV CA-53W ($99.99) based on the Casio CA-53W calculator watch. All include a custom nylon band and Fitbit Air sensor bracket.

Q
How long does shipping take?

7 to 10 business days. We build each watch to order, so there is a short lead time before it ships. Shipping is free.

It fits all wrist sizes. The nylon band is adjustable and works on both smaller and larger wrists.
